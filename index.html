OUR REPORT

About this project

Bringing attention to an issue affecting Ifelodun residents.

Environmental challenges have become an important concern for Ifelodun Community, affecting the surroundings, public health, livelihoods, and the overall quality of life of residents. Understanding these challenges is essential to recognizing their impact and identifying the need for responsible environmental practices. This project examines the environmental conditions in Ifelodun through visual reporting, direct observation, and interviews with residents. By presenting the issues and evidence gathered, the project seeks to raise awareness, highlight the experiences of community members, and encourage informed discussion towards a cleaner, healthier, and more sustainable environment..

Project location: Ifelodun Community

What our report examines

Understanding the environmental challenge.

1

The problem

Ifelodun Community faces environmental challenges, including improper waste disposal, blocked drainage systems, poor maintenance, and inadequate attention from government authorities. These conditions contribute to an unhealthy and poorly maintained environment, affecting residents' safety, public health, and quality of life. Through visual reporting, observation, and interviews with residents, this project documents these challenges and highlights the need for better waste management, regular drainage maintenance, and greater government responsibility.

2

The impact

Improper waste disposal, blocked drainage, and poor environmental maintenance affect the cleanliness and quality of life in Ifelodun Community. These conditions create unpleasant smells, make the surroundings an eyesore, and can expose residents to health risks. They also make the environment less comfortable and pleasant to live in, highlighting the need for better sanitation and regular environmental maintenance.

3

Possible responses

Addressing these challenges requires collective responsibility. Residents should adopt proper waste disposal practices and avoid dumping refuse in drainage channels. Regular waste collection, drainage clearing, and maintenance of public spaces should be encouraged. Government authorities should provide adequate sanitation services, enforce environmental regulations, and respond more actively to the community's needs. Community clean-up exercises can also help promote a cleaner and healthier Ifelodun.
